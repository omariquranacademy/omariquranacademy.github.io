Privacy Policy — Omari Asaan Deen

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Omari Asaan Deen is a free Islamic learning app by Omari Islamic Academy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

The app does not require any account or sign-up, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools operated by us.

Location

If you use the prayer-times feature, the app may use your device location (only with your permission) to request prayer times for your area from the Aladhan API. Your location is sent to that service only to calculate the prayer times; we do not store it.

Third-party services

The app loads its content from trusted third-party services, which may process technical data such as your IP address when your device connects to them:

  • Aladhan API (aladhan.com) — prayer times and Hijri calendar.
  • AlQuran Cloud (alquran.cloud) — Quran text, translations, and recitation audio.

Your use of those services is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for children learning about Islam with their parents. Because the app collects no personal information, no child's personal information is collected, stored, or shared by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
