About Omari Islamic Academy

A premier online institution dedicated to making Quranic education accessible to Muslims worldwide.

Our Story

Guided by Sacred Purpose

Founded by Muhammad Hamza Omari, Omari Islamic Academy is a premier online institution dedicated to making Quranic education accessible to Muslims worldwide.

Our mission is to provide high-quality, personalized Quran learning experiences through one-on-one live classes with qualified scholars. We serve students across the UK, USA, Canada, and over 20 countries.

With a focus on proper Tajweed, Hifz memorization, and foundational Arabic reading, we guide students of all ages on their sacred journey with patience and dedication.
