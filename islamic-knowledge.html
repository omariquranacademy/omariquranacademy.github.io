Daily Sunnah Supplications

A complete, authentic collection of daily Sunnah duas — with Arabic text, transliteration and translation.

Purification

Purification

Dua at Start of Wudu (Ablution)

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ
Bismillahi walhamdu lillahi.
I begin with the name of Allah, and all praises are due to Allah.
Purification

Dua While Performing Wudu

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي وَوَسِّعْ لِي فِي دَارِي وَبَارِكْ لِي فِي رِزْقِي
Allahummaghfir-lee zambi wa wass'ee lee fi dari wa barik-lee fi rizqi.
O Allah, forgive my sins, expand my home for me, and bless my livelihood.
Purification

Dua on Completion of Wudu

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ، اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِنَ التَّوَّابِينَ وَاجْعَلْنِي مِنَ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
Ash-hadu alla-ilaha illallahu wahdahu la shareeka lahu, wa ash-hadu anna muhammadan abduhu wa rasooluhu, Allahummaj-alni minat-tawwabina wa ja'alni minal mutatahhireen.
I bear witness that none has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad ﷺ is His slave and Messenger. O Allah, make me among those who turn to You in repentance, and make me among those who are purified.
Purification

Dua Before Entering the Washroom

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ
Bismillahi allahumma inni a'uzu bika minal khubsi wal khaba'is.
In the name of Allah. O Allah, I seek refuge with You from all evil and evil-doers.

Prayer

Prayer

Dua After Hearing the Adhan

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ هَذِهِ الدَّعْوَةِ التَّامَّةِ، وَالصَّلَاةِ الْقَائِمَةِ، آتِ مُحَمَّدًا الْوَسِيلَةَ وَالْفَضِيلَةَ، وَابْعَثْهُ مَقَامًا مَحْمُودًا الَّذِي وَعَدْتَهُ، إِنَّكَ لَا تُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ
Allahumma rabba hazihid da'watit taammah wassalatil qa'imah, aati Muhammadanil waseelata walfazeelah, wab'asuhu maqamam mahmoodanil lazee wa'adtah, innaka laa tukhliful mee'aad.
O Allah, Lord of this perfect call and established prayer, grant Muhammad the intercession and favor, and raise him to the honored station You have promised him. Verily You do not neglect promises.
Prayer

Dua After Salah (Remove Worry)

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الرَّحْمَنُ الرَّحِيمُ، اللَّهُمَّ أَذْهِبْ عَنِّي الْهَمَّ وَالْحُزْنَ
Bismillahillazi la-ilaha illa huwar rahmanur-raheem, allahumma azhib annil hamma wal huzn.
In the name of Allah, besides whom there is none worthy of worship, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, remove from me worry and depression.

Mosque

Mosque

Dua Before Entering the Mosque

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillahi wassalatu wassalamu alaa rasoolillah, allahum-maftah lee abwaba rahmatik.
In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Mosque

Dua When Leaving the Mosque

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Bismillahi wassalatu wassalamu alaa rasoolillah, allahumma inni as'aluka min fazlik.
In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, I ask You of Your bounty.

Dua Before Eating Food

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَعَلَى بَرَكَةِ اللَّهِ
Bismillahi wa'ala barakatillah.
In the name of Allah and with the blessings of Allah I begin (eating).
Food & Drink

Dua When You Forget to Recite Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ
Bismillahi awwalahu wa aakhirahu.
In the name of Allah at the beginning and at the end.
Food & Drink

Dua After Eating Food

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
Alhamdulillahil lazee at'amana wa saqana wa ja'alana minal muslimeen.
Praise be to Allah who has fed us and given us drink and made us Muslims.
Food & Drink

Dua When Eating at Someone's House

اللَّهُمَّ أَطْعِمْ مَنْ أَطْعَمَنِي وَاسْقِ مَنْ سَقَانِي
Allahumma at'im man at'amani wasqi man saqani.
O Allah, feed the one who fed me and give drink to the one who gave me drink.
Food & Drink

Dua When Drinking Milk

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَنَا فِيهِ وَزِدْنَا مِنْهُ
Allahumma barik lana fihee wazidna minhu.
O Allah, bless it for us and give us more of it.
Food & Drink

Dua After Drinking Water

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي سَقَانَا عَذْبًا فُرَاتًا بِرَحْمَتِهِ وَلَمْ يَجْعَلْهُ مِلْحًا أُجَاجًا بِذُنُوبِنَا
Alhamdulillahil lazee saqana azban furatam bi-rahmatihi wa lam yaj'alhu milhan ujajam bi-zunubina.
All praise is due to Allah, Who by His mercy has granted us sweet and pleasant water to drink and did not make it bitter and salty due to our sins.

Fasting

Fasting

Dua Before Breaking the Fast (Iftar)

اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ
Allahumma laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa alayka tawakkaltu wa ala rizqika aftartu.
O Allah, I fasted for You and I believe in You and I put my trust in You and I break my fast with Your sustenance.
Fasting

Dua After Breaking the Fast

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
Zahabaz zama'u wabtallatil urooqu wa sabatal ajru inshallah.
Thirst has gone, the arteries are moist, and the reward is sure if Allah wills.

Sleep & Waking

Dua Before Sleeping

اللَّهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
Allahumma bismika amutu wa ahya.
In Your name O Allah, I live and die.
Sleep & Waking

Dua When Lying Down to Sleep

بِاسْمِكَ رَبِّي وَضَعْتُ جَنْبِي وَبِكَ أَرْفَعُهُ، فَإِنْ أَمْسَكْتَ نَفْسِي فَارْحَمْهَا، وَإِنْ أَرْسَلْتَهَا فَاحْفَظْهَا بِمَا تَحْفَظُ بِهِ عِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ
Bismika rabbi waza'tu jambi wa bika arfa'ahu fa'in amsakta nafsi farhamha wa in arsaltaha fahfazha bima tahfaz bihi ibadakas saliheen.
In Your name my Lord, I lie down and in Your name I rise. So if You take my soul then have mercy upon it, and if You return my soul then protect it as You protect Your righteous servants.
Sleep & Waking

Dua on Waking Up

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ
Alhamdulillahil lazee ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin nushoor.
All praise is for Allah who gave us life after having taken it from us, and unto Him is the resurrection.
Sleep & Waking

Dua When Unable to Sleep

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ غَضَبِهِ وَعِقَابِهِ وَشَرِّ عِبَادِهِ وَمِنْ هَمَزَاتِ الشَّيَاطِينِ وَأَنْ يَحْضُرُونَ
A'uzu bikalimatil-lahit tammati min ghazabihi wa iqabihi wa sharri ibadihi wa min hamazatish shayateeni wa ayyahzuroon.
I seek refuge with the complete words of Allah from His anger and His punishment and the evil of His slaves, and from the evil suggestions of the devils and from their presence.
Sleep & Waking

Dua When Having a Bad Dream

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ وَشَرِّ هَذِهِ الرُّؤْيَا
A'uzu billahi minash-shaitani wa sharri hazihir ro'uya.
I seek refuge in Allah from Satan and the evil of this vision.

Knowledge

Dua for Beneficial Knowledge

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا
Allaahumma 'innee 'as'aluka 'ilman naafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbalan.
O Allah, I ask You for knowledge that is of benefit, a good provision, and deeds that will be accepted.

Allaahumma 'innee 'as'alukal-jannata wa 'a'oothu bika minan-naar.
O Allah, I ask You for Paradise and seek Your protection from the Fire.
General

Dua for the Deceased (Janazah)

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا. اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ أَحْيَيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَأَحْيِهِ عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، وَمَنْ تَوَفَّيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَتَوَفَّهُ عَلَى الْإِيمَانِ
Allahummaghfir li hayyinaa wa mayyitina wa shahidina wa gha'ibina wa sagheerina wa kabeerina wa zakarina wa unsana. Allahumma man ahyaytahu minna fa'ahyihi 'alal islam wa man tawaffaitahu minna fatawaffahu 'alal iman.
O Allah, forgive our living and our deceased, our present and our absent, our young and our old, our males and our females. O Allah, whoever You keep alive of us, keep him alive upon Islam, and whoever You cause to die, let him die in a state of faith.

Step-by-Step Hanafi Method of Prayer

Salah is the second pillar of Islam and the first deed we will be questioned about.

Fard Prayer

1

Niyyah & Takbir (Intention & Opening)

Face the Qiblah. Make your intention in your heart for the specific prayer you are about to perform. Then raise both hands so your fingertips are in line with your ears, palms facing outward, and say:

2

Sana (Opening Supplication)

Lower your hands and place your right hand over your left on your chest. According to the Hanafi madhab, recite the opening supplication (Sana):

3

Ta'awwudh & Tasmiyah

Recite the seeking of refuge, then Bismillah:

4

Surah Al-Fatihah

Recite Surah Al-Fatihah in every unit (Rak'ah), followed by any short Surah (e.g. Surah Al-Ikhlas):

5

Ruku (Bowing)

Say Allahu Akbar and bow, placing your hands on your knees. In this position recite (three times):

6

Qawmah (Rising from Ruku)

Begin rising while saying the first line, then once you are standing say the second:

7

First Sujood (Prostration)

Say Allahu Akbar and prostrate. Recite (three times):

8

Jalsah (Sitting Between Two Sujoods)

Sit up while saying Allahu Akbar. While sitting, recite:

9

Second Sujood

Return to prostration while saying Allahu Akbar and recite again (three times):

10

Second Rak'ah (Unit)

Stand up while saying Allahu Akbar, placing your right hand over your left on your chest. Repeat Steps 3–9 (Ta'awwudh, Surah Al-Fatihah, another short Surah, Ruku, and both Sujoods) to complete your second Rak'ah, then sit for the Tashahhud.

11

Tashahhud

After the second Sujood, sit back. In the Hanafi madhab, raise your right index finger when you reach the Shahadah. Recite:

12

Salawat (Durood)

Continue sitting and recite Salawat upon the Prophet ﷺ:

13

Supplication Before Salam

Recite a Sunnah supplication:

14

Salam (Ending the Prayer)

Turn your head to the right and say the Salam, then turn to the left and repeat it. This completes the prayer.

Witr Prayer

1

First Rak'ah

Pray the first Rak'ah exactly as in any Fard prayer: Takbir, Sana, Ta'awwudh, Surah Al-Fatihah and a short Surah, then Ruku and the two Sujoods. Then stand up for the second Rak'ah.

2

Second Rak'ah & Tashahhud

Pray the second Rak'ah like the first: Surah Al-Fatihah and a short Surah, Ruku, and the two Sujoods. Then sit for the Tashahhud (At-tahiyyatu lillah…). After the Tashahhud, stand up for the third Rak'ah — do NOT give the Salam yet.

3

Third Rak'ah — Recitation

Standing, recite Surah Al-Fatihah followed by a short Surah (e.g. Surah Al-Ikhlas).

4

The Extra Takbir of Witr

While still standing, say Allahu Akbar and raise both hands up to the ears, then fold your hands back on your chest. This extra Takbir is the special sign of the Witr prayer.

5

Dua-e-Qunoot (Before Ruku)

With your hands folded on your chest, recite Dua-e-Qunoot. In the Hanafi madhab this is recited BEFORE the Ruku, while standing.

6

Ruku & Sujood

Saying Allahu Akbar, go into Ruku and recite 'Subhana Rabbiy-al-'Adheem' (3 times). Rise saying 'Sami' Allahu liman hamidah / Rabbana lakal hamd'. Then perform the two Sujoods as in normal prayer.

7

Tashahhud, Salawat & Salam

Sit for the Tashahhud, recite Salawat (Durood) and a supplication, then end with the Salam — first to the right, then to the left, exactly like the end of the Fard prayer.

Janaza Prayer

1

First Takbir — Sana & Fatihah

Stand in rows facing the Qiblah. Raise both hands up to the earlobes saying the first Takbir (Allahu Akbar), then fold your hands on the chest. Recite the Sana (Subhanaka Allahumma…), then Ta'awwudh, and Surah Al-Fatihah followed by a short Surah.

2

Second Takbir — Salawat (Durood)

Say the second Takbir (you may raise the hands again or keep them folded — both are permissible). Recite Salawat (Durood Ibrahim) — the same as recited at the end of the regular prayer.

3

Third Takbir — Dua for the Deceased

Say the third Takbir (hands remain folded). Recite the supplication for the deceased, such as:

4

Fourth Takbir — Short Supplication

Say the fourth Takbir (hands remain folded). You may make a short general supplication for yourself, the deceased, and all Muslims. Then pause briefly.

5

Tasleem (Ending)

End the prayer by saying the Salam once to the right ('As-salaamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah'). Giving the Salam to both sides, as in a regular prayer, is also permissible. There is no Ruku and no Sujood in the Janazah prayer — it is performed entirely standing.
